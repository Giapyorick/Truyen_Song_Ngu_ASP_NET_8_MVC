"Those who came to my house to read the lesson before the ceremony, stand up!"
The man in the priest's cassock glared and shouted at the students.
His face was bloated with fat, and a massive crucifix hung around his neck.
His small, slanting, yet fierce eyes stared intently at the six young students, four boys and two girls, who had just stood up.
The children looked up at him with timid, frightened glances.
He pointed at the schoolgirls and commanded.
"Those girls over there, sit down."
The young girls sat down, letting out a long sigh of relief.
Father Vasily's sly, snake-like eyes kept staring fixedly at the remaining schoolboys who were still standing.
"Come closer, my dear children."
He pushed his chair back, stood up, and rushed toward the little ones who were huddled together.
"Which of you smokes, you little rascals?"
All four replied in a tiny, muffled voice.
"Father, none of us smokes."
The priest's face turned completely red with rage.
"You don't smoke, you scoundrels!"
"Then who threw tobacco into the dough for the communion bread?"
"None of you smokes?"
"Fine, let me see."
"Turn out your pockets!"
"Hurry up!"
"I said: Turn out your pockets!"
Three of them began to pull out all the items from their pockets and display them on the table.
The priest examined every single inch of the pocket linings, trying to find a remaining trace of tobacco.
Finding nothing, he turned to question the fourth child, a black-eyed boy wearing a gray coat and blue pants with patches on the knees.
"And you, why are you just standing there like a statue?"
The boy, his eyes full of resentment, grumbled in reply.
"Father, my pants don't have pockets."
And his hand touched the seam along the waistband of his pants.
"Ah, so you claim you don't have pockets?"
"Do you think I don't know which mischievous brat ruined my dough?"
"Do you honestly think I will let you keep studying here this time?"
"No, you little brat, I will not let you get away with this."
"The other day, your mother begged and pleaded, so I showed mercy and did not expel you."
"But this time, there is no way."
"Get out of the classroom!"
And he ruthlessly grabbed the boy by his ear, throwing him out onto the porch.
